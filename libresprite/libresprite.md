## **Why we do not offer support for LibreSprite**
LibreSprite is an **outdated **and **buggy **fork of Aseprite with a different codebase and developer community. It was forked using a loophole in the licensing agreement which has since been updated to better reflect the Aseprite team's intentions.

Many bugs that occur in LibreSprite have long been fixed in current Aseprite versions, and are **redundant **to bring up here. The differences between the two pieces of software make it difficult or impossible for our team to troubleshoot.

We recommend finding a dedicated LibreSprite forum for assistance instead. Alternatively, you can **[purchase Aseprite](<https://aseprite.org>)**, or you can **[compile it](<https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/INSTALL.md>)** as a free alternative so we can offer our help.
