## Can I use Aseprite for high resolution art?
While it is possible, it should be noted that Aseprite is **not optimized for high resolutions**. Large canvases, particularly in combination with higher frame and/or layer counts, can cause the software to **lag, crash, or cause files to corrupt**.

Artists using Aseprite as a high-res digital art or animation software may want to consider other options. Most digital art programs offer pixel brushes.

Otherwise, **regular saving** and the maintenance of **backups **are highly recommended.
