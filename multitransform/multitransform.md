**How do I transform and move selections across multiple different layers or frames?**

1. Make your selection on your canvas, on any layer or frame. It's crucial that you do this **first**.
2. Select the Layers or frames that you want to alter down in your layer tab using SHIFT + Left Click. It is crucial you do this **after** your canvas selection.
3. Move and transform as needed. **As you transform your selection, your preview will only show your current layer or frame**. It will look like the other frames and layers aren't being transformed. This is a purely visual bug. **Your transformation is working.**
4. Deselect. After Deselecting, your transformation gets applied and you will see the changes to all your layers and frames.
