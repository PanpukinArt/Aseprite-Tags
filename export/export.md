## How to export your sprites larger
You're probably reading this because you posted your pixel art at its original size, only for it to be tiny, illegible and blurry on Discord. Here's how to fix that!

1. Go to your original Aseprite file
2. Go to File ⇨ Export ⇨ Export As
3. In the "Resize" option, your export is currently set to 100%. Choose a higher percentage, typically 300-600% works (the smaller your sprite, the higher the percentage should be)
4. Choose where to export your file to by pressing the three dots next to "Output File", then "Choose File"
5. Click "Export". Now you can post your new file legibly!

Be sure to keep your current file so you can always adjust your art at its original pixel scale later.
